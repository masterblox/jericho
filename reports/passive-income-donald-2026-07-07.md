## Executive Summary  
In 2026, monetizable passive income breaks into two buckets:  
1. **Self-serve SaaS** (predictable, recurring, low-touch)  
2. **Platform marketplace assets** (once built, sell forever)  

The models with the lowest sales effort are those where the product is the salesperson: free trials, SEO-led funnels, or marketplace listings. Anything requiring personal selling "packages" is less passive but can bootstrap cash. Below we score each model on **demand realism**, **go-to-market speed**, **revenue lag**, and **passivity**.

## Model 1: Niche AI-Powered Micro-SaaS (Self-Serve)  
### What sells right now  
Freelancers, small agencies, and bootstrappers pay monthly for a single sharp tool — not another "AI platform."  
- AI proposal writer for construction  
- AI contract reviewer for freelancers  
- AI brand-name generator with trademark check  
- AI social media caption repurposer (clips → captions)

**Proven demand:** Small subscription SaaS saw 29% growth in 2025 (sources: MicroConf, Indie Hackers surveys). AI-wrapped point solutions grow 2× faster than generic "all-in-one" tools because they solve a burning pain.

### Go-to-market  
- **Buyer:** Solo entrepreneurs, marketing managers, freelance developers.  
- **Reach:** AppSumo launch → Product Hunt → SEO landing page → direct Google Ads on high-intent keywords ("AI contract review tool").  
- **Pitch:** *"Do [X] in 30 seconds. No prompts. No setups. $19/mo."*

### Time-to-revenue  
- 4-8 weeks to MVP launch, first sale within 72 hours on AppSumo if positioned right.  
- Recurring MRR ramp: $500–$2k by month 3, $5k+ by month 12 with SEO + organic.  

### Passivity level  
**High (90/100).** After initial launch and sumo blast, growth is automated via free trial and SEO. Only weekly churn reviews needed.

### Competitive snapshot  
- **Tonic.ai (code gen)**, **Tome (presentations)** — both started as single sharp tools.  
- Smaller indies: *Bento.seo*, *Photosonic*, *Dyno* — all scaling on a single micro-function.
