## 2. SkillCenter Paper (arXiv 2607.07676v1)

**Published:** Jul 8, 2026
**Authors:** Tianming Sha, Yue Zhao, Lichao Sun, Yushun Dong
**Links:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07676v1) | [Code](https://github.com/LabRAI/SkillCenter) | [Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Tommysha/skillcenter-bundles)

**Key numbers:**
- 216,938 structured skills across 24 domain bundles
- 114,565 source-grounded skills (peer-reviewed journals, arXiv, 24,000+ technical sources)
- 102,373 community skills (GitHub, ClawHub marketplace)
- Ships as offline-searchable SQLite FTS5 bundles

**Architecture:** Multi-source acquisition -> SkillGate (LLM-based quality gate) -> template-driven generation -> iterative source-grounding -> quality-controlled publishing. Source grounding means every claim maps to an exact quotation in its source.

**Relevance to Mechanica fleet:**
- Directly competitive/adjacent to our skill system. Same FTS5 + skill bundling approach we use.
- SkillGate quality pipeline is novel — we don't have a systematic quality gate for skills.
- Source grounding (traceability) is something our skills lack entirely.
- The 24 domain bundles could be a source of pre-built skills to evaluate/import.

**Action:** Review the HuggingFace dataset. Evaluate whether SkillGate pipeline could augment our curator system.

## 6. Agon: Competitive Cross-Model RL (arXiv 2607.07690v1)

**Published:** Jul 8, 2026
**Summary:** Two competing models grade each other's reasoning. Both attempt the same problem; in alternating roles, one drafts a solution and the other reads it while solving. Each is rewarded for out-solving the other. No process labels, no reward model — reasoning is judged implicitly during training.

**Relevance:** Technique advancement. If this approach works, it could inform how we do agent self-improvement and evaluation. The "adversarial improvement" pattern mirrors how we should think about agent quality loops.

## 7. Institutional Red-Teaming (arXiv 2607.07695v1)

**Published:** Jul 8, 2026
**Key finding:** Deployment rules causally alter collective safety — changing only the consequence rule moves mean fatality by 22-58 percentage points within every population.

**Relevance:** Regulatory/public-positioning signal. As we scale fleet autonomy, institutional rules (not just model safety) are the dominant safety factor. This paper provides ammunition for responsible deployment messaging.
